Legal Desk for Jira

The front desk of your legal team: a Rovo agent that qualifies any colleague's legal request, scores the risk and files a complete Jira ticket.

Install on your Atlassian site

Atlassian Forge app · no outbound network access: requests stay in your Jira site.

Try it in 2 minutes

  1. Click Install, pick your Jira site on the Atlassian page and confirm.
  2. Open Rovo chat, choose the Legal Intake agent and try “A customer is asking us to sign an NDA”.
  3. Open the ticket it files: the Legal context panel shows the risk score, the reasons and the lawyer's checklist; the Legal register project page lists every request.

No setup needed: requests go to a project whose name contains “Legal” if you have one, otherwise to the first project the app can see.

Use Legal Desk from any AI agent (MCP)

Legal Desk exposes its tools through the Atlassian MCP server, so Claude, Cursor or your own agent can score a legal request, file it in Jira, list the legal queue and look up a request.

  1. A site admin enables the tools once: admin.atlassian.com → Apps → Sites → your site → Connected apps → Legal Desk → View app details → Details → Tools → Enable tools.
  2. Connect your MCP client to https://mcp.atlassian.com/forge/57922510-a15e-4ff2-b93b-e69ea12ff147 and sign in with your Atlassian account.
  3. Add the open-source skill so your agent follows the right workflow and guardrails: Legal Desk skill on GitHub · SKILL.md · zip for Claude (MIT).
