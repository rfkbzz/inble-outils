Privacy policy — Legal Desk for Jira

Legal Desk runs entirely on Atlassian Forge (Runs on Atlassian). It makes no external network calls and has no server of its own.

What the app reads

Jira issues of the legal request project (summary, labels, due date, status) to build the Legal register and the Legal context panel.

What the app stores

One issue property per request (legal-desk-request: request type, amount, urgency, personal-data and outside-EU flags, counterparty name) stored inside your own Jira site. Nothing is copied outside Atlassian. We do not store accounts, emails or any personal data of your users.

Rovo agent

The Legal Intake agent processes what colleagues type in Rovo chat according to Atlassian's Rovo terms. Legal Desk only receives the structured fields the agent passes to its actions.

Deletion

Uninstalling the app removes its access. Issue properties remain on your issues and can be deleted with the Jira REST API.

The app provides triage and routing, not legal advice.
